## Assessing authorities and classification

First confirm the designated authority and applicable instrument through Home Affairs. Common official sources include [VETASSESS](https://www.vetassess.com.au/), [Engineers Australia](https://www.engineersaustralia.org.au/), [ACS](https://www.acs.org.au/), [Trades Recognition Australia](https://www.tradesrecognitionaustralia.gov.au/), [AITSL](https://www.aitsl.edu.au/), [AHPRA](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/), [CPA Australia](https://www.cpaaustralia.com.au/), [CA ANZ](https://www.charteredaccountantsanz.com/), and [IPA](https://www.publicaccountants.org.au/).

[Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/) may support occupation classification or labour-market context, but proves a migration rule only when it explicitly publishes one or an official instrument adopts it.
