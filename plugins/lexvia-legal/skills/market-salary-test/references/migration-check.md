# Conditional AMSR checks

Read only for an explicit migration, sponsorship, AMSR or visa request. A public salary recommendation from a job title does not establish all employer-specific salary requirements.

## Verify the current framework

Consult and cite current primary sources on each run:

- [Home Affairs salary requirements](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/employing-and-sponsoring-someone/sponsoring-workers/nominating-a-position/salary-requirements)
- [AMSR methodology instrument, latest compilation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2018L00284/latest)
- [Migration Regulations 1994, latest compilation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/latest), using the nomination provisions relevant to the requested subclass/stream
- [Home Affairs labour market testing](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/employing-and-sponsoring-someone/sponsoring-workers/nominating-a-position/labour-market-testing), only to explain the distinction if needed

Check effective dates and whether a rule applies to the nomination's lodgement/decision date. Do not hard-code thresholds, exemptions or historical methodology. Salary thresholds and AMSR are distinct checks, and salary components must be assessed under the applicable rules. A survey's base-pay figure is not automatically the same as eligible guaranteed annual earnings.

## Select the applicable method

Check whether an Australian citizen or permanent resident performs equivalent work at the same workplace and location, and whether an applicable industrial instrument exists. Apply the current legal method for that combination. Where employment documents are required, public salary surveys do not replace them. Do not assume there is no equivalent worker merely because the user supplied none.

Determine the relevant award/agreement and classification before using its rates. Where the current method allows market information alongside an industrial instrument, check the applicable minimum. If no equivalent worker or applicable instrument exists, support the appropriate market-evidence method with relevant original sources.

When employer circumstances, worker equivalence, visa subclass/stream or required pay records are unavailable, continue the title-based benchmark and list the outstanding information. Do not require these details merely to produce the public report. State that the formal AMSR assessment remains incomplete.

## Keep three results separate

1. **Observed market evidence and suggested base salary:** the researched occupation/role benchmark.
2. **Industrial minimum:** the verified award/agreement obligation, if determinable.
3. **Visa salary requirements:** applicable AMSR method, eligible earnings, income threshold and any verified exemption or concession.

Do not raise the observed market range to a visa threshold and call the higher number the market rate. If an independently determined AMSR fails an applicable threshold, a higher offer alone may not satisfy the nomination requirements; explain the issue using the current primary rules. Do not assume bonuses, superannuation or non-cash benefits can cover a shortfall.

The conclusion should identify what the public report supports and what remains to be checked. Do not certify a nomination, promise approval, or claim that searching existing job ads completes labour market testing.
